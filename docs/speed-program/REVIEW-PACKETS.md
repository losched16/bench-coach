# Review packets: skater step, half-circle cut, indoor falling-start substitute

Three items need a qualified coach. For each: what it is, the safety case, what I checked and what
I could not, and a recommendation. **A recommendation is not an approval.** None of the three is in the
drill library, and none has a video.

---

## Packet 1. Skater Step and Stick

| | |
|---|---|
| Where it appears | Session C, every level. Step-only until the hop is unlocked. Foundation can never unlock the hop (Move to Development first) |
| Full draft write-up | `DRILL-DRAFTS.md`, draft 1 |
| Dose | Development 2 × 3 each side, 45–60 s rest. Foundation 1 × 3. Advanced as Development |
| Library status | No library drill exists. The reference that used to be attached was removed (it showed hurdle hops) |

**Safety case.** Lateral landing control is protective practice for a fielder or runner, but a single-leg
landing loads the knee. The design keeps the load low: a step, a quiet landing, a two-second hold; no hop
until two sessions of controlled landings; one harder step a week; stop for knee, ankle or hip pain or a
knee that caves inward.

**Checked:** the gate in code (hop locked until `skater_hop`, Foundation cannot unlock it, pain blocks every
advance), the rest and volume numbers against the template, the draft wording against the safety design.
**Not checked:** how the movement looks when done, by a real child. No video. No coach has watched it.

**Questions for the coach**
1. Is a two-second hold the right length for the age band, or should younger players hold one?
2. Is "knee over the middle of the foot" the right cue, and are the stop rules enough?
3. Is two sessions of controlled landings the right bar before a hop?
4. Should a surface or footwear note be added (the draft says dry, flat, non-slip)?

**My recommendation:** add to the library only after a coach edits and approves the wording and watches a
child do it. Until then keep it as a program-only exercise labelled "No library drill yet".

## Packet 2. Half-Circle Cut (Round the Cones)

| | |
|---|---|
| Where it appears | Session B, every level (Foundation 2–3 short starts) |
| Full draft write-up | `DRILL-DRAFTS.md`, draft 2 |
| Dose | Development 2 each direction, 45–60 s rest. Week 1 effort 70–85% |
| Library status | Closest existing drill is "Plant and Go — The 45-Degree Cut" (marked partial) |

**Safety case.** Cutting is where ankles and knees are lost on bad footing. The design keeps week-one
effort at 70–85%, requires dry flat ground, tells the coach to stop on slipping or loss of form, and never
adds speed and a reaction cue in the same week (one harder step per week).

**Checked:** the 70–85% effort note is the specification's own for Week 1; the one-harder-step rule is
enforced in code. **Not checked:** whether three cones at three yards is the right size for each age, or how
the plant looks in practice.

**Questions for the coach**
1. Is three yards (two indoors) the right cone spacing?
2. Is "plant the outside foot" the right instruction for every age, or too technical for Foundation?
3. Is two each direction enough for Foundation, which the template raises to "2–3 short starts" total?

**My recommendation:** keep the "closest library drill" label; do not add a new library drill until
a coach has run it with players.

## Packet 3. Indoor falling-start substitute (20 × 15 ft)

| | |
|---|---|
| Where it appears | Session A, only when the space is "indoor" |
| Full analysis | `INDOOR-FALLING-START.md` |
| Text shown | "Lean-and-catch. Lean forward from a standing stance, take two quick low steps (about 2 yards) and land balanced on two feet. Use the 20 ft length and keep 10 ft clear past the stop line. Grippy footwear, dry non-slip floor. If the floor is slick or the space is tight, skip it and add one wall-drive set instead." |
| Source | Assistant proposal. The source gives an indoor substitute only for the 10-yard acceleration, not the falling start |

**Safety case.** The outdoor falling start is 5 yards; the indoor room's short side is exactly 15 ft (5
yards) with no run-out. The substitute removes the long run and the uncontrolled fall, keeps the lean, ends in
a planned two-footed landing, names the floor and footwear conditions, and offers an explicit way out.

**Checked:** that the text can no longer prescribe a 5-yard start indoors (a test). **Not checked:** the
numbers. 2 yards and 10 ft clear are mine.

**Questions for the coach**
1. Are 2 yards and 10 ft clear right, or should they be different?
2. Four reps at the lower speed, or fewer?
3. At Foundation, show it indoors at all, or default to the wall-drive fallback?

**My recommendation:** use the wall-drive fallback at Foundation indoors until a coach says otherwise.
